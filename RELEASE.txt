DIGICOMP4 - Digimon World 4 Recomp - PS2 USA - v0.2.0-preview

Extract the ZIP and run DigimonWorld4.exe.
Place your own Digimon World 4 (USA).iso or disc1.iso beside the EXE, or choose
the ISO on first launch. No Python/compiler needed. Game data is not included.
Windows 10/11 x64; GPU mode needs Direct3D 11.3 rasterizer ordered views.

16:9 automatically enables widescreen. F1 opens settings; F6 switches 4:3/16:9.
GPU 1x, FXAA and sharp UI sampling are the default. F5/F9 save/load states.
Restart after camera or renderer changes; older states retain camera matrices.
Far clip 100-200% is experimental and does not change object LOD or streaming.

Validated: opening, character menus and entry into the initial hub.
Observed locally: menu about 59-60 FPS, initial hub about 30 FPS.
Full-game graphics, combat, sound and extended distance remain under validation.

Source and updates: https://github.com/gorigamia/DIGICOMP4
